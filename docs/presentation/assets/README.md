# Presentation assets

The portrait in `shemar-marks.png` was supplied by Shemar Marks for this presentation. The original pixels are unchanged. PowerPoint applies a circular crop on the cover.

The EC2, Systems Manager, S3, CloudWatch and SNS symbols, plus the general user, server and database symbols, come from the [official AWS architecture icon package](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/) released July 31, 2026. Their colors and proportions are preserved. The resource SVG originals are retained, with faithful PNG exports for sharp presentation rendering. The diagram labels and connections remain editable.

The original recovered app, notification email and teardown screenshots remain in `evidence/`. They provide the lab evidence. The supporting repair image and background below are illustrations, not evidence of a real repair shop, Proxmox console or AWS environment.

## Generated illustrations

Both illustrations were created with the built-in image-generation tool. The final assets are `repair-workbench.png` and `teal-background.png`. The presentation uses a white reading area over the background's lower-left corner so the ornament does not interfere with text.

### Repair workbench prompt

> Create a premium editorial photograph for an infrastructure portfolio case-study presentation about a small electronics repair shop. Landscape 3:2 composition. Close overhead-oblique view of a real-looking open laptop under repair on a clean light-gray workbench. Visible motherboard, screwdriver and precision tools thoughtfully arranged, a paper repair slip with absolutely no readable writing, no people and no screens with UI. Restrained photographic style, natural daylight, cool teal and blue-gray tones, tactile brushed-metal details, believable components, selective focus. Use the main open laptop in the center-right and tools on the left, tightly framed without excessive empty background. This is an illustrative photograph, never evidence of a real lab. No cloud icons, no floating objects, no glowing cyberpunk graphics, no logos, no readable text, no watermark.

### Background prompt

> A sophisticated, subtle background artwork for a 16:9 white and teal technology case-study PowerPoint. Canvas 1920x1080, crisp pure white #FFFFFF across 90 percent of the canvas including the whole center and left reading area. Sparse small charcoal and deep teal halftone dot clusters ONLY tucked into the extreme top-right corner and bottom-left corner. One very thin cyan-teal angular circuit trace in the far right margin, with a tiny square terminal, plus a narrow pale teal geometric patch cut by the extreme lower-right edge. Swiss editorial design, clean precise layout, flat vector print aesthetic rendered as bitmap, no gradients, no glow, no text, no logos, no icons, no photography. Most of the canvas must be perfectly blank white to hold editable titles and diagrams.
